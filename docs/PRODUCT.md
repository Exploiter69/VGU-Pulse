# VGU Pulse Product Contract

## Vision

Make VGU Pulse the place VGU students open to answer:

> **What's happening at VGU, and who can I do it with?**

Pulse is a campus-native network, not a generic social-media clone.

## Core loop

```
useful campus information
        ↓
open Pulse
        ↓
participate
        ↓
see campus response
        ↓
share / invite
        ↓
more students participate
        ↓
denser campus network
        ↓
return
```

## First value

A new student should be able to open Pulse and immediately see:
- something important
- something happening today
- one low-friction interaction
- one student/community signal

The first interaction should take seconds, not require a profile-building session.

## Initial product surface

### Home
- important official information from VGU-Signal
- today's events/opportunities
- daily poll
- student questions
- selected community/social content

### Participate
- polls
- ratings
- questions
- event interest/RSVP
- predictions and lightweight campus games

### Discover
- events
- clubs
- opportunities
- campus resources
- communities

### People
Planned after the initial network has density:
- profiles
- interests
- branch/year context
- study/project/activity matching
- friends/connections
- privacy-controlled presence

### Social
Introduced gradually and moderated:
- confessions
- memes
- hot takes
- anonymous questions
- topic discussions

### Connect
Later:
- study/project groups
- activity matching
- mutual anonymous messaging
- temporary voice rooms

### Dating
An opt-in product mode, not the foundation:
- 18+ only
- mutual matching
- block/report
- privacy controls
- no unsolicited anonymous contact

## Trust model

Every information item has a source class:
- **Official** — directly published by VGU or an authorized source.
- **Verified** — independently verified by the Pulse/Supply pipeline.
- **Student-reported** — submitted by a student and not independently verified.
- **Unverified** — visible only where product policy permits it.

Pulse must never imply that student-generated content is an official VGU statement.

## Network effects

The product should create value from campus density:
- polls become more useful as participation grows
- events become more useful as attendance/interest grows
- questions become more useful as more students can answer
- people discovery becomes more useful as more students join
- communities become more useful as activity accumulates

Invitations should be contextual: share a poll, event, question, result, community, or activity rather than demanding generic invites.

## Retention

The first retention loop is not streaks.

Students should return because:
- something changed
- a poll is live
- an event is approaching
- someone answered their question
- a community has activity
- a campus result is worth seeing

Gamification is optional reinforcement, never the reason the product exists.

## Product principles

1. Campus density beats feature count.
2. Utility should bring students in; social interaction should make them stay.
3. Low-friction participation beats complex onboarding.
4. Pseudonymity is not a promise of absolute anonymity.
5. Moderation must scale with social features.
6. Private data must remain private.
7. No fake activity, fake users, or manufactured engagement.
8. Do not build a feature merely because another social network has it.
